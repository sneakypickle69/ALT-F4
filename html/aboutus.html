ABOUT US

We're gamers, just like you. ALT F4 was born out of one simple frustration — finding the right gaming gear shouldn't feel like a side quest.

ALT F4 is a gaming-focused online retail store built for every kind of player. Whether you're a casual gamer or a hardcore enthusiast, we've got you covered.

We believe no gaming product should be out of reach. That's why we stock everything from the widely known to the wildly obscure, all in one place:

  • Mainstream gaming gear & peripherals
  • Niche & hard-to-find products
  • Barely available items
  • Controllers, keyboards, headsets & more

No endless searching across a dozen sites. No settling for "close enough." Just you, your setup, and everything you need to play the way you want.
